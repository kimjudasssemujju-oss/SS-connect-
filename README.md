Love Has No Borders.

SS Connect is an international dating and friendship platform under SS Network.

Our mission is to help adults aged 18 and above around the world build meaningful connections, serious relationships and friendships.

Who We Welcome

- Straight and LGBTQ+ adults.
- People from Africa, Asia and every region of the world.
- Adults seeking dating, love, long-term relationships or friendship.

Planned Features

- Secure registration and login.
- Personal profiles and profile photos.
- Match preferences and discovery.
- Private messaging between matched users.
- Blocking, reporting and safety moderation.
- Optional premium memberships in the future.

Our Values

- Inclusion and respect.
- Privacy and security.
- Authentic connections.
- A safer community for everyone.

Under SS Network

SS Connect is currently under development.

Our slogan: Love Has No Borders. Meet. Connect. Belong.
